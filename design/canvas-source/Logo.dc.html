02 · Logo system

A drop, a person, a ripple.

Every mark ships as optimized SVG with no raster, in full color, single color (also as currentColor), white-on-blue and dark. Wordmarks are outlined from Readex Pro Medium and hand-spaced.

The drop

A soft teardrop on an 18-unit circle with a rounded crown. The source everything flows from.

The person

Head and shoulders cut in negative space. The shoulders open through the base, so the person rises out of the water.

The ripple

One ring, the front half of a 24 × 5.6 ellipse in Lagoon. In motion it becomes the loader and the success ripple.

Clear space and minimum size
x x
  • x = ½ symbol height. Keep x clear on every side, ring included.
  • Symbol: 16 px minimum (micro cut up to 24 px).
  • Horizontal lockup: 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print.
  • Stacked lockup: 72 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
Do and don't
Do place on white or Mist 50.
Do use the white mark on Mist 600 to 950.
Do switch to the AR lockup in RTL interfaces.
Do use the micro cut at 24 px and below.
Don't stretch or squash.
Don't recolor outside the four variants.
Don't rotate or add shadows and outlines.
Don't place full color on mid-tone blue.
Co-branding with the customer's logo
  • The company logo (uploaded in the setup wizard) always leads. Mawred is the quiet endorser.
  • "powered by" mark: micro symbol + wordmark at 40–50% of the company logo's height, in Ink 600 or the single-color variant.
  • Never lock the two marks together or tint the company logo.
  • Company logo slot: 160 × 40 px max in the sidebar, 180 × 48 px max in email headers. Contain, never crop.
