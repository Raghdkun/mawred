11 · People, avatars and certificate

Everyone at work, drawn with respect.

Character rules
  • Faceless and flat. No eyes or mouths, so no expression can read as mocking or gendered.
  • Five skin tones, four hair colors, mixed in every scene; no scene shows only one tone.
  • Everyday workwear: blazers, shirts, sweaters, cardigans and long dresses. No religious or regional dress.
  • Neutral poses: holding a phone or tablet, waving, pointing, cheering. No physical contact between figures.
  • No religious or national symbols, no flags, no text in the art.
  • Dark mode keeps trousers and dark garments a step lighter than the navy so figures never vanish.
